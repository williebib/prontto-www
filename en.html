VOC Ltd. connects Italian original manufacturers with processing customers in Taiwan and the region, supplying synthetic rubber and chemical raw materials through a single point of contact for quotation, scheduling and after-sales coordination.

About us

VOC Ltd. is the Taiwan agent of Versalis, the chemicals business of the Italian ENI Group. We import and supply synthetic rubber and related chemical raw materials, and we coordinate orders end to end.

From enquiry, quotation and contract confirmation to container planning, shipment scheduling and after-sales quality handling, customers deal with one contact instead of several intermediaries.

What we do

Supply and import

Stable supply of synthetic rubber, including Versalis EUROPRENE grades, and related chemical raw materials.

Quotation and lead time

Pricing, contract confirmation, container and shipment planning through a single contact.

Technical support

Grade selection, specification review and sample requests matched to your process.

After-sales handling

Quality issue reporting, investigation and coordination with the plant to protect long-term supply.

The Italian connection

Versalis is the chemicals arm of the ENI Group, headquartered in Italy, and one of the leading European producers of synthetic and thermoplastic rubber. As the Taiwan agent we work directly with the commercial, quality and logistics teams at the plant, which means:

  • Factory-direct pricing, availability and technical documentation, with fewer intermediaries
  • Specification and quality issues are resolved directly with the QA team at the Italian plant
  • Support for full-container imports and batch traceability required by processors
